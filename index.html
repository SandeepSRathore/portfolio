02 About

I'm a senior Java and full stack engineer based in Bangalore, ten years into building enterprise web applications, microservices and REST APIs. Most of that time has been spent in places where downtime is expensive — datacenter infrastructure management, banking, and healthcare.

A lot of my best work has been modernisation rather than greenfield. I led a team of four through a full stack upgrade of Dell's OpenManage Enterprise platform — Hibernate 5 to 6, Spring 5 to 6, Spring Boot 2 to 3, Java 11 to 17, and the whole javax to jakarta namespace migration — which improved performance by 20–30% and closed out most of the platform's open security vulnerabilities.

More recently I've been putting LLMs into production rather than demos: Spring AI, MCP, RAG and vector search. I built an automated defect resolver that takes a Jira ID, pulls the details over MCP, performs root cause analysis and proposes a fix, and won Dell's PAN India Agentic AI Hackathon with OME Assist. I'm a Certified Scrum Master, have run Scrum for teams of six to eight, and hold a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering.
