02 About

I'm a software developer who likes problems with a correct answer — the kind where you can write the test first and know when you're done. Most of my work has been backend: APIs, data pipelines, and the infrastructure that keeps them upright.

I care about code that the next person can read. That usually means fewer abstractions than feel clever at the time, names that say what they mean, and tests that describe behaviour rather than implementation.

Outside of work I read about distributed systems, maintain a few small open-source tools, and am always happy to talk through a gnarly architecture problem.
